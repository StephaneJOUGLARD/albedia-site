    Calculator method and sources
    • Climate zone: in France, derived from the department, according to the H1, H2 and H3 zoning used in the CEE sheets.
    • Estimated consumption: floor area × average consumption for the type of building (French Sustainable Real Estate Observatory, OID, 2025 barometer, 2024 data): offices 126 kWh/m²/year, non-food retail 110, logistics warehouses 65, unheated storage 19 (agriculture). In the absence of published data, industry uses the warehouse value and public buildings the office value. Replace it with your actual consumption if you know it.
    • Share of air conditioning: ALBEDIA assumption of 15% of the consumption of a fully air-conditioned building in zone H1, 20% in zone H2 and 30% in zone H3 (Switzerland 15%, Italy and Spain 30%), half if only part of the building is air-conditioned.
    • Savings: 20% of the air conditioning of the top storey, under the roof. This is the average gain found by the US EPA across more than 25 studies, ranging from 2 to 40% depending on the building. For a roof that is already grey or light-coloured, the gain is halved (ALBEDIA assumption). The slight extra heating cost in winter is not deducted: according to the US EPA, it is generally offset by the summer savings, because the sun is lower and the days shorter.
    • CEE bonus (France only): sheet BAT-EN-112, i.e. 160, 170 or 270 kWh cumac per m² of treated roof in zone H1, H2 or H3, valued at €8.70 per MWh cumac (forward price index for standard CEEs, June 2026, French Ministry for the Ecological Transition). The amount paid depends on the funder. Conditions: tertiary building in commercial use, roof above an enclosed air-conditioned volume, heating and cooling by heat pump, no existing reflective coating, product with a solar reflectance index (SRI) above 100 when new and 90 when aged, works carried out by a professional.
    • Price per kWh: €0.20 excluding VAT by default, to be adjusted to your contract. Benchmark: €0.25 excluding VAT for small business customers in France in the first half of 2026 (Eurostat).

    Sources: US EPA (winter penalty) · sheet BAT-EN-112 · CEE price indices · OID 2025 barometer · Eurostat prices (sources in French, except the US EPA)
