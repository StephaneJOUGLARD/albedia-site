About us

ALBEDIA is the brand of CGBI, a subsidiary of GROUPE NOVELIA, dedicated to reflective roofs for professional buildings. It draws on the group’s experience in real estate and construction.

Our name

ALBEDIA, as in albedo

Albedo is the share of sunlight that a surface reflects. A dark roof has a low albedo: it absorbs radiation and turns it into heat. A light-coloured, reflective roof has a high albedo: it sends the sun back to the sky.

Our business comes down to this word: increasing the albedo of roofs so that buildings absorb less heat, and the people who work in them have a more bearable summer.

albedo

n. — Fraction of solar energy reflected by a surface, from 0 (everything is absorbed) to 1 (everything is reflected).

Our mission

Buildings that stay liveable in the summers to come

Episodes of intense heat are becoming more frequent. For companies and public authorities, they weigh on staff comfort, the storage of goods and air-conditioning bills. Acting on the roof is one of the simplest measures to deal with them: no demolition, no work inside, and on the very surface that receives the sun.
