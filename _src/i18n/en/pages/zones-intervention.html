Europe

Rules and incentives by country

French energy-saving certificates (CEE) do not apply outside France. Each country has its own rules, which we take into account in the study.

France

The CEE bonus BAT-EN-112 can fund part of the works for a tertiary building in commercial use, air-conditioned by heat pump. Tertiary buildings of 1,000 m² and more must also reduce their energy consumption under the tertiary decree. And since 1 July 2025, every employer must prevent heat-related risks during episodes of intense heat.

Source: sheet BAT-EN-112. Our pages in French: CEE bonus · tertiary decree · heat at work.

Switzerland

In Switzerland, measures concerning energy consumption in buildings are primarily the responsibility of the cantons, under the Federal Constitution. Requirements and any support therefore vary from one canton to another.

Source: Federal Constitution, Article 89.

Italy

The Italian decree on minimum energy performance requirements of 28 October 2025, in force since 3 June 2026, requires the use of materials with high solar reflectance to be assessed for the roofs concerned: at least 0.65 for a flat roof and 0.30 for a pitched roof.

Source: Infobuildenergia (in Italian).

Spain

Spain created an energy-saving certificate system in 2023, by Royal Decree 36/2023 of 24 January 2023. Whether a roofing project is eligible for this system is checked case by case.

Source: Boletín Oficial del Estado (in Spanish).

France

In France, three climate zones

The CEE sheets divide mainland France into three climate zones. The zone of your building sets the amount of the BAT-EN-112 bonus: it is highest in zone H3, where cooling needs are greatest.
