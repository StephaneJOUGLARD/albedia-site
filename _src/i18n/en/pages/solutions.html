Our method

How a project unfolds

  1. 1

    Roof visit and survey

    Measurement of the area, type and condition of the surface, slope, rainwater drainage, ponding areas, rooftop equipment (smoke vents, rooflights, ducts, panels) and safe access conditions. We also note how the building is used: heated, air-conditioned, ventilated.

    {{icon:document}}You receive a visit report.

  2. 2

    Study and quote

    We assess the value of a cool roof for your building, in summer and winter, and choose the system suited to the surface. We check whether the project is eligible for incentives, such as the CEE bonus BAT-EN-112 in France, and propose a schedule that fits your activity.

    {{icon:document}}You receive a detailed quote, item by item.

  3. 3

    Surface preparation

    Cleaning of the roof, repair of defects (cracks, blisters, corrosion spots), treatment of upstands and roof details, protection of equipment and of elements to be preserved.

  4. 4

    Application of the reflective system

    Bonding primer where the surface requires it, then coats of reflective coating at the specified thicknesses. The works are carried out within the temperature and humidity ranges set by the manufacturer, with marking and protection suited to work on roofs.

  5. 5

    Acceptance and follow-up

    Inspection of the work and of product consumption, then handover of the completion file: technical data sheets, SRI values of the applied product and maintenance sheet. We offer you a maintenance contract to limit soiling and preserve the reflectance.

    {{icon:document}}You receive the completion file.

Compatible roofs

Which roofs do we work on?

A cool roof can be applied to most flat and low-pitched roofs. Compatibility is always confirmed during the survey, depending on the system chosen.

Cases to rule out or deal with first

  • Roofs where water ponds, for reflective paints.
  • Surfaces that are too damaged: the waterproofing must be renewed before any coating.
  • Fibre cement sheets that have not been surveyed: an asbestos survey is essential before any work (in France, it is mandatory for buildings constructed before 1 July 1997).
  • Metal deckRibbed metal roofs, new or old
  • Bituminous waterproofingBituminous membranes in good condition
  • Synthetic waterproofingPVC, TPO or EPDM membranes, depending on compatible systems
  • ConcreteFlat roofs and slabs
  • Fibre cementAfter an asbestos survey
  • Mixed roofsSeveral surfaces on the same building, each treated with its own primer

The system

What we apply to your roof

  1. 1

    The existing surface, prepared

    Cleaned, repaired and treated at roof details.

  2. 2

    The primer

    Chosen to bond to the material: metal, bitumen, synthetic membrane or concrete.

  3. 3

    The reflective coating

    One or more light-coloured, emissive coats, whose SRI is measured when new and after ageing.

After the works

Maintenance, to keep the performance

Dust, pollen and deposits gradually reduce reflectance, especially in the first year. Regular maintenance, suited to the coating, keeps the roof light-coloured and effective.

At acceptance, we give you a maintenance sheet. We also offer a maintenance contract: inspection visit, cleaning in line with the manufacturer’s recommendations and minor repairs if needed.
