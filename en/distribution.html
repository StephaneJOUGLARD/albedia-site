Cool roofs for shops and sales floors

Shops and supermarkets are almost always air-conditioned: every unit of heat that does not come in through the roof is one the air conditioning does not have to remove.

Request a quoteHow a cool roof works

The challenge

Air conditioning working against the sun

A sales floor combines a large roof, lighting, refrigeration equipment and many visitors. In summer, the air conditioning runs at full power to keep customers and staff comfortable.

By limiting the solar gains through the roof, a cool roof reduces the cooling load. In France, this is exactly the case covered by the CEE scheme BAT-EN-112, which can fund part of the works.

Buildings concerned

  • Supermarkets and hypermarkets
  • Specialist stores and showrooms
  • Shopping arcades and retail parks
  • Click-and-collect points and adjoining stockrooms

What you gain

What a cool roof does for you

Air conditioning under less strain

Less heat to remove and lower power peaks during heatwaves.

More comfort in store

For customers and staff alike, especially under poorly insulated roofs.

Works that can be funded

In France, under conditions, the CEE bonus BAT-EN-112 can cover part of the investment.

Before the works

What we look at

Every building has its own constraints. The roof visit is used to identify them before we propose a system and a quote.

See our method
  • The heating and cooling system: in France, sheet BAT-EN-112 requires a heat pump system
  • Rooftop equipment: chillers, air handling units, ducts
  • Organising the works outside opening hours if necessary
  • The fire performance of the roof for buildings open to the public

Incentives and rules

What may apply to your building

In France, CEE sheet BAT-EN-112 can apply if your shop is a tertiary building in commercial use, with the roof above an air-conditioned volume and heating and cooling by heat pump. Premises of 1,000 m² and more are also subject to the tertiary decree. In the other countries, we review the incentives available for your project.
